# Basic Math, Parity and Divisibility

> A large fraction of A/B problems are solved by a small observation about parity, ceilings, or sums.

## What to learn

- Parity (odd/even) arguments, divisibility rules, remainders.
- Ceil division: `(a + b - 1) / b` for positive integers.
- Arithmetic series `n(n+1)/2`, sum of squares, min/max reasoning.
- Find patterns by brute-forcing small inputs, then prove the pattern.
- Digit manipulation: sum of digits, reversing numbers, converting bases.

- Integer division rounding for negatives.
