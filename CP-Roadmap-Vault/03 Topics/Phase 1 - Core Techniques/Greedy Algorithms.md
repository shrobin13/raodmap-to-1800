## What to learn

- Exchange argument: swapping two neighbors never makes the answer worse.
- Sort by a key and process (earliest deadline, smallest first, interval end time).
- Priority-queue greedy (always take the best available now).
- Interval scheduling and covering problems.
- Verify with a brute force on small inputs before trusting a greedy.
