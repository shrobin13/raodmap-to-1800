# Video Channels

- **Errichto** (YouTube): beginner-friendly algorithm explanations, stress testing and contest tips.
- **Algorithms Live!** (YouTube): problem walkthroughs and lectures.
- **Neal Wu** (YouTube): contest streams and live problem solving.
- **Codeforces round editorial videos**: many rounds have official video editorials.

Use videos for concepts; do not replace problem solving with watching ([[Practice Method]]).
