# Upsolving Guide

Upsolving is solving the contest problems you could not solve during the contest, after reading hints or editorials.

## Steps

1. For each unsolved problem you were close to, try again for 30 minutes with no editorial.
2. Read the first hint only. Try again.
3. Read the editorial. Close it. Re-implement from scratch.
4. Write one line in [[Mistake Log]]: what was the key idea you missed?
5. Redo the problem after 2-3 days to check retention.

## What to upsolve

- Everything up to one problem above your best solved problem in that contest.
- Any problem with a topic from [[Weak Spots]].
