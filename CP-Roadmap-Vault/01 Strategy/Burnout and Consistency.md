# Burnout and Consistency

- Take one full rest day per week.
- A bad contest changes nothing about your long-term trend. Look at the 3-month average, not the last round.
- When stuck for a week: switch to easier problems for 2 days to rebuild confidence.
- Track streaks in [[Rating Tracker]] but do not obsess over them.
- Practice with friends or a community (see [[Platforms Hub]]); explaining solutions out loud reveals gaps.
