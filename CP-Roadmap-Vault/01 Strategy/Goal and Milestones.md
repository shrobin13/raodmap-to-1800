# Goal and Milestones

**Goal:** reach a Codeforces rating of 1800 (Expert) by December 2027.

This is roughly 14 months from a beginner. It is ambitious but realistic with steady daily practice, rated contests and consistent upsolving. Rating growth is not linear: expect plateaus and drops, especially around 1200 and 1500.

## Principles

1. Solving problems and contests beats reading tutorials. Ratio: about 60% solving, 20% learning, 20% contests and upsolving.
2. Practice at the edge of your level (about 100-200 rating above current).
3. Upsolve every contest. Most rating growth happens after the contest ends.
4. Consistency beats intensity. Two hours every day beats ten hours on Sunday.
5. Only core topics are required for 1800; optional and stretch topics can wait.
