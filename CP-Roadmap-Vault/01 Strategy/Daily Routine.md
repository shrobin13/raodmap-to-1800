# Daily Routine

A sample 2 hour block (adjust to your time):

| Minutes | Activity |
|---|---|
| 10 | Review yesterday's notes in [[Mistake Log]] |
| 30 | Learn or revise one concept from the current topic note |
| 60 | Solve problems from the topic practice plan |
| 10 | Write a short entry in [[Problem Log]] |
| 10 | Read an editorial for something you could not solve |

## Weekly rhythm

- Mon-Fri: topic work.
- Saturday: contest (live or virtual) using [[Contest Playbook]].
- Sunday: [[Upsolving Guide]] and [[Weekly Review Template]].

If you only have 45 minutes: do one problem at your level and write one line in the log. Never skip two days in a row.
