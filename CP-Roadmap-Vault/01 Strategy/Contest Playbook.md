# Contest Playbook

## Before the contest

- Sleep, water, food. Open your template and test it compiles.
- Know the format: Div 4, Div 3, Educational, Div 2 ([[Platforms Hub]]).
- Warm up with one easy problem if you have 20 minutes.

## During the contest

1. Read all problems A-C quickly (about 5 minutes). Solve in order of difficulty, not alphabet.
2. Spend at most 15 minutes on A, 25 on B, 40 on C before reconsidering.
3. Before submitting: check constraints, edge cases, and long long.
4. On Wrong Answer: re-read the statement, then test small and extreme cases. Do not resubmit blindly.
5. On Time Limit Exceeded: recompute complexity, then look for a better idea.
6. Do not look at the standings obsessively.

## After the contest

- Record results in [[Contest Log]].
- Upsolve using [[Upsolving Guide]].
- Add new ideas to [[Mistake Log]] and [[Weak Spots]].
