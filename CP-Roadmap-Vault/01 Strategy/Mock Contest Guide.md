# Mock Contest Guide

A mock contest is a virtual rated contest done under full contest conditions. It is the highest-leverage activity in Phase 4's consolidation month — it bridges the gap between topic practice and real performance.

## When to use this

- From Phase 3 onwards, one mock per week on Saturday replaces or supplements a live round.
- December 2027 (consolidation month): do 2-3 full mock weeks.
- Any time your rating stagnates but you feel you "know" the topics.

## How to run a mock contest

1. Go to [Codeforces](https://codeforces.com/) → **Gym** or past round → **Virtual Participation**.
2. Choose a contest matching your current level: Div 2 for 1200-1700; Educational for mixed practice.
3. Set a timer, close all notes and tabs (no cp-algorithms, no VisuAlgo).
4. Follow the [[Contest Playbook]] exactly as you would in a real round.
5. Submit under virtual judgment — the system will give you the same feedback as the real contest.

## After the mock

1. Record: problems solved, time per problem, what you froze on.
2. Immediately upsolve using [[Upsolving Guide]] — do not wait until the next day.
3. Fill in [[Contest Review Template]] and [[Contest Log]].
4. Ask: *"Which one change would have gained me the most points?"* Write it in [[Mistake Log]].

## Choosing the right difficulty

| Your current rating | Choose |
|:---|:---|
| <1200 | Div 4 or Div 3 from the last 6 months |
| 1200-1500 | Div 3 or Educational Codeforces Round |
| 1500-1700 | Educational or Div 2 from 12+ months ago |
| 1700+ | Div 2 from 18+ months ago |

Avoid very recent rounds — editorials spread quickly and you may have seen the problems.

## Measuring improvement

After 4 weeks of mocks, compare:
- Average problems solved per contest
- Average time to solve problem A and B
- Whether you attempt C within the time window

These numbers should trend up. If not, revisit [[Weak Spots]] and [[Practice Method]].
